Ecosystems compete for the same builders. The people who know first stay quiet.

Judges, mentors and senior developers see promising teams long before anyone announces them. Saying so in public exposes them and leaks the lead to every rival. Senyap lets them back a team with a stake and a reason, and stay unnamed the whole way through.
